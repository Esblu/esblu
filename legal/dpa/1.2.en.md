This document supplements the [Terms of Use](/podmienky-pouzivania) and governs the processing of personal data under Art. 28 GDPR in cases where your company (as the controller of its own customer/company data) uploads to Esblu documents, photos, or records that contain personal data of third parties (e.g., employees, customers, suppliers). In such cases, your company is the controller of that data, and Esblu (Esblu s. r. o., Company ID (IČO): 57 815 941) acts as processor with respect to it.

For personal data directly connected to your own user account (login email, management of company members, security logs, communication with support, billing), Esblu is always the controller — this relationship is governed by the [Privacy Policy](/ochrana-osobnych-udajov), not this document.

## 1. Subject matter and duration of processing

Esblu processes personal data uploaded by your company solely for the purpose of providing the service's features (storage, display, AI recognition, and management of records) for the duration of your company account or until the relevant data is deleted.

## 2. Nature and purpose of processing

Storage, hosting, display, search, and — for documents and photos submitted for AI recognition — also making the content available to the AI provider (OpenAI) for the purpose of data recognition. Esblu does not use data uploaded in this way for any purpose other than providing the service to your company.

## 3. Type of personal data and categories of data subjects

The scope is determined solely by your company, depending on what is uploaded to Esblu — typically this may include names, contact details, vehicle registration numbers (license plate/VIN), photos, signatures on documents, and information on invoices or service records. Data subjects may in particular be employees, customers, or business partners of your company.

## 4. Processing on instructions

Esblu processes personal data solely on documented instructions from your company — in practice, the use of a particular feature of the app (e.g., uploading a document for AI recognition, saving a record) already constitutes such an instruction, including any instructions concerning the transfer of data outside the European Economic Area. If Esblu believes that an instruction infringes the GDPR or another data protection regulation, it will inform your company without undue delay.

## 5. Confidentiality

Esblu ensures that persons authorized to process personal data (including Esblu itself, where it processes data personally) are bound by confidentiality, whether by law or by contractual obligation.

## 6. Security measures (Art. 32 GDPR)

Access to data in the database is restricted through authentication and row-level security rules, so that users only see data belonging to their own company. Files in storage are protected by access policies; some files are public (e.g., the company logo), while others are accessible only via temporary signed links. A detailed security summary forms part of the internal documentation prepared prior to the start of production operation.

## 7. Subprocessors

The current list of further subprocessors used by Esblu is set out on the [List of subprocessors](/subprocessors) page. By using Esblu, your company grants general authorization for their use to the extent set out in that list.

If Esblu plans to engage a new subprocessor or replace an existing one, it will inform your company in advance (for example, by updating the List of subprocessors page and/or providing a notice within the application) and will allow a reasonable period to object. If your company objects to the change for legitimate reasons related to data protection, the parties will agree on a solution; if no agreement is reached, your company may proceed under Article 12 (termination and deletion/return of data).

Esblu contractually binds every subprocessor it engages to the same data protection obligations that Esblu owes to your company under this document, to the extent appropriate to the nature of the processing carried out by that subprocessor.

## 8. Assistance with data subject rights

Esblu provides your company with reasonable technical and organizational assistance in responding to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights under Art. 12 to 23 GDPR (e.g., access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection), insofar as these concern personal data processed on behalf of your company.

## 9. Assistance with security, incident notification, and DPIA

Esblu will notify your company without undue delay of any incident concerning personal data you have uploaded to Esblu, and will provide the information your company needs in order to fulfil its own notification obligations under Art. 33 and 34 GDPR (notification to the supervisory authority and, where applicable, to data subjects). Esblu will also provide reasonable assistance with a data protection impact assessment (DPIA, Art. 35) and any prior consultation with the supervisory authority (Art. 36), where your company needs this in connection with processing carried out through Esblu.

## 10. Information and cooperation with audits

Esblu will make available to your company the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with the obligations set out in this document and Art. 28 GDPR, and will allow for and contribute to reasonable audits or inspections carried out by your company or a third party authorized by it, including inspections — subject to reasonable prior notice and without prejudice to the security or confidentiality of the data of other Esblu customers. In deciding on the form of an audit, Esblu may take into account relevant certifications or independent audits available to it.

## 11. International transfers

If a subprocessor processes data outside the European Economic Area, a valid legal transfer mechanism must be used (e.g., standard contractual clauses). The current status for each provider is set out on the [List of subprocessors](/subprocessors) page — at the time this document was published, the exact processing location of certain providers is marked as TODO for verification.

## 12. Termination, deletion, or return of data

Upon termination of the provision of the service (e.g., closure of the company account), Esblu will, at your company's choice, either delete or return all personal data processed on its behalf, and will delete existing copies, unless European Union or Member State law requires further retention.

## 13. Obligations and rights of your company as controller

Your company is responsible for ensuring that instructions given to Esblu (including the act of uploading data itself) comply with the GDPR, that it has a valid legal basis for processing the personal data of third parties that it enters into Esblu, and that it has fulfilled its own information obligations toward the data subjects. Your company has the right to information and audit under Article 10 and the right to object to the engagement of a new subprocessor under Article 7.

## 14. Relationship to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses

This document is based on the requirements of Art. 28(3) and (4) GDPR and is informed in substance by the standard contractual clauses between controllers and processors under Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/915 of 4 June 2021 — it does not constitute a verbatim adoption of that template as a whole. Where your company requires this (e.g., for reasons of internal compliance policy), it may agree with Esblu on the formal adoption of the clauses under Decision 2021/915 in their original form as an annex to this document.
