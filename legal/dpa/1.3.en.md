## ANNEX NO. 2 TO THE DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT Technical and Organisational Measures

### 1. Introductory Provisions

**1.1** This annex forms an integral part of the Data Processing Agreement concluded between the Controller and the Processor and defines the technical and organisational measures adopted by the Processor to ensure an appropriate level of protection of personal data pursuant to Art. 28(3)(c) and Art. 32 of the Regulation.

**1.2** The technical and organisational measures under this annex apply to the processing of personal data by means of the Esblu Application to the extent set out in Annex No. 1 to this Agreement and relate in particular to its web and mobile interface, database environment, related technical services and integrations provided by the Processor.

### 2. Technical and Organisational Measures

- **Area:** Technical and organisational measures
- **Access rights management:** Access to the data and functionalities of the Esblu Application is controlled by means of user roles and assigned permissions. The scope of access depends on the User's affiliation with the Customer's account, the User's role and the available functionalities of the Application.
- **User authentication:** Login to the Application is provided by the Supabase Auth service by means of an e-mail address and password. In the web interface, login via a Google account is also available. Passwords are managed by the authentication service and stored in cryptographically secured form.
- **Segregation of Customers' data:** Access to data is tied to the relevant Customer account and the User's permissions. The Application applies access permission checks in order to prevent unauthorised disclosure of data between individual Customers.
- **Administrator access:** Technical administration of the Application, the database environment and related services is performed through administrator accounts of the respective providers. Access to customer data on the Processor's side is intended for necessary operational, service, support and security purposes in accordance with the Agreement.
- **Encryption of data in transit:** Communication between the User and the web interface of the Application is secured by means of HTTPS. Communication with database and external application services uses secured HTTPS interfaces and the relevant transport encryption mechanisms.
- **Protection of stored data:** The database and file storage of the Application is provided by means of the Supabase service, which uses mechanisms for encryption of data at rest and protection of the database environment.
- **Protection of documents and attachments:** Documents, photographs and attachments are stored in private Supabase Storage. Access to them is subject to verification of the User's permissions. Time-limited signed links valid for no more than one hour are used to display files.
- **Cloud infrastructure:** The operation of the Application is provided by means of the providers Vercel and Supabase. The production database and file storage of Supabase and the Vercel server functions use the Frankfurt am Main region in Germany. Processing by means of other external services is governed by the terms of the Agreement and Annex No. 3.
- **Automated processing and AI:** When AI functions are used, the data necessary to perform the relevant functionality, in particular document recognition, transcription of a voice input or processing of a text prompt, are sent to an external provider. The User reviews and confirms data obtained through document recognition before they are definitively saved.
- **Internal communication and notifications:** Internal communication between Users is provided through the functionalities of the Application using the Supabase service. Access to communication and attachments is subject to user permissions. System notifications by default do not contain attachments or the full content of documents.
- **Technical logs and diagnostics:** The operation of the Application uses technical logs and diagnostic mechanisms of the providers Supabase and Vercel. For selected operations, the Application records User identifiers, time data and related operational information.
- **Source code and change management:** The source code of the Application and its changes are managed by means of a version control system. The operation and deployment of the Application are provided by means of the Vercel cloud infrastructure and related development tools.
- **Data export and erasure:** The Application enables authorised Users to export selected records and to perform erasure operations according to the available functionalities and assigned permissions. For supported file deletion operations, a mechanism for their subsequent removal from storage is used. Obligations upon termination of processing are governed by Article X of the Agreement.
- **Notification of security incidents:** The contact address [privacy@esblu.com](mailto:privacy@esblu.com) is designated for communication concerning personal data protection and security incidents. Notification of personal data breaches to the Controller is governed by Article VI of the Agreement.

### 3. Updating of Measures

**3.1** The Processor is entitled to change or replace individual technical and organisational measures taking into account technological developments, changes in the services used and security risks, provided that such a change does not result in a reduction of the overall level of protection of personal data under this Agreement.
