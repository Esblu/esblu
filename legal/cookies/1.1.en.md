This "Cookie information" section follows on from the [Privacy Policy](/ochrana-osobnych-udajov) (part "Use of cookies and similar technologies"). It lists the technologies that the Esblu website and application (web, installed web app and mobile app) actually store on or read from your device.

## Summary

- Esblu **does not set any cookies** on its domain.
- Esblu **does not use** analytics, advertising, marketing or tracking tools, session-recording tools, or third-party scripts and embedded content (e.g. Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, YouTube, maps). Fonts are part of the application and are loaded from the Esblu domain.
- We only use the browser's local storage (localStorage, sessionStorage), a service worker for push notifications and, in the mobile app, the device's secure storage — solely for sign-in, security, features you requested, and remembering settings you chose yourself.
- Because we do not use any optional analytics or marketing technologies, **we do not show a cookie consent banner**. If this changes in the future, such technologies will only be activated after obtaining your consent, and this page will be updated.

## Strictly necessary technologies

- **`sb-<project>-auth-token`** — localStorage (web); encrypted device storage in the mobile app (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). Provider: Supabase Auth. Purpose: sign-in and keeping your session. Retention: until sign-out; session validity is set by the authentication service.
- **`sb-<project>-auth-token-code-verifier`** and related keys `…-flow-…-code-verifier`, `…-flows-code-verifier` — localStorage / encrypted device storage. Provider: Supabase Auth. Purpose: one-time security code (PKCE) when signing in with a Google account. Retention: until sign-in completes or sign-out.
- **`esblu.oauthPending.v1`** — localStorage. Provider: Esblu. Purpose: completing sign-in with a Google account (returning to the app, a pending invitation, confirmation of documents at registration). Retention: at most 15 minutes; deleted after use and on sign-out.
- **`esblu.partnerPrefill.v1`** — sessionStorage. Provider: Esblu. Purpose: one-time hand-over of business partner details you gave the assistant to the form. Retention: deleted once read, at most 10 minutes; gone when the tab is closed or you sign out.
- **`esblu.dashboard.focus.v1`** — sessionStorage. Provider: Esblu. Purpose: remembering the last opened overview module while you work. Retention: until the tab is closed or you sign out.
- **Service worker `/sw.js`** — browser service worker. Provider: Esblu. Purpose: showing a push notification and opening the right screen when clicked; it does not cache page content. It is registered only after you turn on and allow push notifications. Retention: until notifications are turned off or site data is cleared.
- **Web push subscription** — your browser's push service storage. Provider: your browser's push service (e.g. Google, Mozilla, Microsoft or Apple). Purpose: delivering notifications you turned on. Retention: until you unsubscribe (signing out of the app cancels the subscription).
- **`esblu.push.native.enabled`** and **`esblu.push.installationId`** — mobile app local storage. Provider: Esblu. Purpose: recording that push notifications are on for this device, and a random installation identifier needed to match the device correctly. Retention: the enabled flag until notifications are turned off or you sign out; the installation identifier until the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging installation identifier and registration token** (Android mobile app only) — app storage on the device. Provider: Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging). Purpose: delivering push notifications you turned on in Android. Retention: managed by Firebase; turning notifications off cancels the registration.

## Settings you choose yourself

These items only store your own choice in the app interface; they are not used for tracking and are not sent to third parties.

- **`esblu_locale`** — localStorage. Provider: Esblu. Purpose: selected interface language (Slovak, German, English). Retention: until changed or browser data is cleared.
- **`esblu.preferredCompany.<user id>`** — localStorage. Provider: Esblu. Purpose: the company you last selected on this device (access is always checked by the server). Retention: until changed or browser data is cleared.
- **`esblu.dashboard.firstSteps.dismissed.v1:<company id>`** — localStorage. Provider: Esblu. Purpose: hiding the "First steps" card after you close it. Retention: until browser data is cleared.
- **`esblu.chat.bubble.position.v1`** and **`esblu.mobile.chatFab.position.v1`** — localStorage. Provider: Esblu. Purpose: position of the chat button on screen. Retention: until browser data is cleared.
- **`esblu.voiceReplies.v1`** — localStorage. Provider: Esblu. Purpose: turning the assistant's spoken replies on or off. Retention: until changed or browser data is cleared.

## Third-party services on their own domains

When you sign in with a Google account, a Google page is shown on Google's own domain; cookies Google sets there are governed by its terms and Esblu has no access to them. Esblu does not embed any third-party content or scripts in its pages.

## How to remove this data

Signing out deletes sign-in data and one-time security codes. You can remove all other items at any time by clearing site data in your browser settings, or in the mobile app by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. You can turn push notifications off in the app or device settings.
