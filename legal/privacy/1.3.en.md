This policy explains what personal data Esblu may process, why we need it, and what rights users of the free trial version have.

## A. Controller

The controller of the Esblu service is Esblu s. r. o., with its registered seat at Karpatské námestie 10A, 831 06 Bratislava – Rača, Slovak Republic, Company ID (IČO): 57 815 941. For data protection matters, you can contact us at [privacy@esblu.com](mailto:privacy@esblu.com).

## B. What data we process

Depending on which features you use, we may process in particular:

- your registration and login email address,
- the account or company name and company logo,
- data entered in the AI Capture (Inbox), Vehicles, Machinery, and Inventory modules,
- photos and documents submitted for AI processing and the data recognized from them,
- technical data required for login, security, and operation of the service.

We do not process passwords in readable form. Login is secured by the authentication service used.

## C. Purposes for which we use the data

We use the data to:

- create an account, log in, and manage the user account,
- provide and improve the features of Esblu,
- store and display the records created by the user,
- perform AI recognition of data from uploaded documents and photos,
- protect the service, resolve errors, and communicate with the user,
- fulfill legal obligations that apply to us.

## D. Legal bases for processing

We process data primarily in order to create an account and provide the service at the user's request. Where necessary, the basis may also be our legitimate interest in secure operation, detecting misuse, and improving the service, the performance of a legal obligation, or consent, where a specific processing activity requires it. Consent may be withdrawn at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

## E. Processors and data transfers

To operate Esblu we use, depending on the feature involved, Supabase (database, authentication, and storage of documents/photos), Vercel (operation and hosting of the application), and OpenAI (AI processing of uploaded documents and photos, see Section F). Account-related email communication (registration confirmation, password recovery) is sent exclusively by the Supabase authentication service — Esblu does not operate its own email infrastructure and does not use a separate service for bulk or transactional email.

The contact email addresses [info@esblu.com](mailto:info@esblu.com) and [privacy@esblu.com](mailto:privacy@esblu.com) (privacy@esblu.com is an alias pointing to the same mailbox) are hosted with Namecheap (Private Email). If you write to us at these addresses — for example with a question about your account or a request concerning personal data — Namecheap processes the content of that communication (your email address, the message content, and any attachments) to the extent necessary to deliver and store the email.

The current list of all processors, the purpose of processing, and the place of processing can be found on the [List of processors (subprocessors)](/subprocessors) page.

Each processor receives only the data necessary for the relevant feature. The scope and location of processing depend on the specific service and its current configuration. Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, appropriate legal mechanisms and safeguards under applicable rules should be used (e.g., standard contractual clauses) — the specific status for each processor is set out on the List of processors page referenced above.

Where we also act as a processor on behalf of your company with respect to personal data that your company uploads to Esblu (e.g., in documents, photos, or records), the terms of that processing are governed by a separate [Data Processing Agreement (DPA)](/dpa).

## F. AI processing of documents

Documents and photos that the user submits for AI recognition (e.g., via AI Capture (Inbox) or data recognition from a vehicle document) are transmitted, to the extent necessary, to OpenAI for data recognition and pre-filling of records. Esblu transmits only the content of the document/photo needed for recognition, not any further data about the user beyond that. The AI may read data inaccurately, incompletely, or incorrectly — the user must review the result before saving and any further use.

Esblu does not use the recognized result for automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects on the user — the result of AI recognition is always reviewed and confirmed by the user before saving.

## G. User responsibility for submitted data

The user should only upload to Esblu data and documents that they are authorized to process. Where such data concerns other individuals, the user is responsible for having an appropriate legal basis, fulfilling any applicable information obligations, and ensuring the accuracy of the data uploaded.

## H. How long we retain data

We retain data for the duration of the account and, as needed, during use of the service. After account closure or the handling of a request, we may retain data for a reasonable period where necessary to technically complete deletion, to protect legal claims, for security, or to comply with a legal obligation. The specific retention period depends on the type of data and the purpose.

## I. Your rights

Subject to the conditions set out by law, you may request access to your data, its correction, deletion, restriction of processing, and data portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interest and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You can also submit these requests directly in the application via [Settings → Privacy and Data](/nastavenia).

You may also send a request to [privacy@esblu.com](mailto:privacy@esblu.com). Before handling a request, we may reasonably verify your identity. If you believe your rights have been violated, you may file a complaint with the Slovak Data Protection Authority (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky, supervisory authority, [dataprotection.gov.sk](https://dataprotection.gov.sk)).

Esblu does not carry out automated individual decision-making, including profiling, that would produce legal or similarly significant effects on the user.

## J. Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect data, such as access control, separation of user data, and secure transmission. However, no system can be considered completely secure. The user must protect their login credentials and notify us of any suspected misuse of their account.

## K. Changes to this policy

We may amend this policy when the features of Esblu, the processors used, or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page with the date of the last update. In the event of a material change, we may also notify users in an appropriate manner within the application or by email.
