## Rights of the data subject

When personal data are processed, you are in the position of a data subject, i.e. the person whose data we process. As a data subject, you have certain rights in connection with the processing of personal data which enable you to obtain an overview of what data we process about you, to decide on their rectification or erasure, to restrict their use or to object to their processing. Below is an overview of your rights that you may exercise in relation to the processing of your personal data:

- **Right of access:** you have the right to be provided with a copy of the personal data we hold about you, as well as information on how we use your personal data.
- **Right to rectification:** if your personal data are inaccurate, incomplete or out of date, you have the right to ask us to rectify or complete them.
- **Right to erasure (right to be forgotten):** you have the right to ask us to erase your personal data if the conditions laid down by the GDPR are met. In certain cases, however, we may be entitled or obliged to continue to retain certain data, for example to comply with legal obligations or for the exercise and defence of legal claims.
- **Right to withdraw consent:** where we process your personal data on the basis of your consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time. The withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of the processing of personal data carried out before its withdrawal.
- **Right to restriction of processing:** in certain circumstances, you have the right to ask us to restrict the processing of your personal data, for example if you contest their accuracy or the lawfulness of the processing.
- **Right to data portability:** if the conditions laid down by the GDPR are met, you have the right to receive the personal data you have provided to us in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and to transmit them to another controller.
- **Right to object:** you have the right to object to the processing of your personal data which we carry out on the basis of our legitimate interests, on grounds relating to your particular situation.
- **Right to lodge a petition to initiate personal data protection proceedings:** if you believe that the processing of your personal data infringes legal regulations, you have the right to contact the competent supervisory authority, which in Slovakia is the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority in the Member State of your habitual residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement.
- **Right to protection against automated individual decision-making, including profiling:** you have the right, under the conditions laid down by the GDPR, not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing of personal data, including profiling, which produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affects you.

This concerns in particular handling the requests of data subjects, fulfilling accounting and tax obligations, keeping records of documents required by law and the exercise or defence of legal claims.

### Handling data subject requests and recording related actions

In connection with fulfilling obligations under the GDPR, we process personal data necessary for the receipt, assessment and handling of requests of data subjects concerning the processing of their personal data.

For this purpose, we may process data necessary to identify the requester, assess the legitimacy of the request, carry out the relevant action and document the manner in which the request was handled.

If the request concerns personal data that we process on behalf of a Customer as a processor, we will provide the relevant Customer with the necessary cooperation in handling it in accordance with the GDPR and the relevant data processing agreement.

- **Purpose of processing:** Receipt, recording, assessment and handling of requests of data subjects when exercising their rights under the GDPR, verification of the identity of requesters in justified cases, carrying out the requested actions, communication with requesters and documenting the fulfilment of the related legal obligations.
- **Legal basis:** Compliance with a legal obligation pursuant to Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with Art. 12 to 22 GDPR.
- **Categories of data subjects:** Users of the Application, Customers who are self-employed natural persons (sole traders), persons acting on behalf of Customers and other natural persons who exercise rights in connection with the processing of their personal data.
- **Categories of personal data:** First name, surname, e-mail address, where applicable other contact details, user account identifier, data stated in the request and the related communication, type and scope of the right exercised, data necessary for reasonable verification of identity, date of receipt of the request, data on the course of its handling, the actions taken, the decision adopted and the date the response was sent. To the extent necessary, also the personal data to which the specific request relates.
- **Categories of recipients:** Designated persons of the Company, the e-mail provider Namecheap, Inc., and, where necessary to handle the request through the technical functionalities of the Application, also the providers Supabase Pte. Ltd. and Vercel Inc. For requests concerning data processed on behalf of a Customer, also the relevant Customer as controller. In cases laid down by law, also the competent supervisory authority or another authorised public authority.
- **Erasure period:** Personal data processed when handling requests of data subjects are retained while the relevant request is being handled and thereafter for a maximum of 3 years from its final resolution, to the extent necessary to demonstrate compliance with legal obligations. Data and documents whose further retention is not necessary for this purpose are erased without undue delay after the request has been handled. If related proceedings are initiated or a specific legal dispute arises within that period, the data necessary for its resolution are retained until the conclusion of the relevant proceedings and the settlement of the related legal claims.
- **Transfer of personal data to third countries or international organisations:** In electronic communication via the provider Namecheap, Inc. (USA), personal data may be processed in the United States of America. When requests are handled through the technical services of the Application, personal data may also be processed by the providers Supabase Pte. Ltd. (Singapore) and Vercel Inc. (USA), while the main database infrastructure and server functions of the Application are located in Germany. In connection with the provision of these services, personal data may be transferred to Singapore and the USA. To safeguard the relevant transfers, the providers use standard contractual clauses pursuant to Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR.

### Fulfilment of the Company's accounting and tax obligations

In connection with our business activities, we process personal data necessary for keeping accounts, issuing and recording accounting documents, recording payments received and made, and fulfilling other obligations arising from accounting and tax legislation.

For this purpose, we process in particular personal data contained in invoices and other accounting documents, as well as data necessary to demonstrate the business transactions carried out and the fulfilment of the relevant legal obligations.

- **Purpose of processing:** Keeping the Company's accounts, issuing, receiving and recording accounting and tax documents, recording receivables, payables and payments, fulfilling tax obligations and ensuring the retention of accounting documentation in accordance with legislation.
- **Legal basis:** Compliance with a legal obligation pursuant to Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR, in particular in conjunction with Act No. 431/2002 Coll. on Accounting, Act No. 595/2003 Coll. on Income Tax and Act No. 222/2004 Coll. on Value Added Tax, to the extent that the relevant obligations apply to the Company.
- **Categories of data subjects:** Customers who are self-employed natural persons (sole traders), self-employed natural persons (sole traders) providing services or supplying goods to the Company, persons acting on behalf of business partners and other natural persons whose personal data are contained in the Company's accounting or tax documentation.
- **Categories of personal data:** First name, surname, business name of the self-employed natural person (sole trader), address of the registered office or place of business, billing and contact details, identification and tax data of the self-employed natural person (sole trader), bank account number, data on the supply provided, amounts invoiced, payments, receivables and payables and other personal data contained in the relevant accounting and tax documents of the Company.
- **Categories of recipients:** Designated persons of the Company, providers of accounting and tax services, if the Company uses their services, banks and the relevant public authorities, in particular the financial administration authorities. When accounting documents are sent or received electronically via the Company's e-mail mailbox, also the e-mail provider Namecheap, Inc.
- **Erasure period:** Personal data contained in accounting documents and other accounting records covered by Section 35(3)(a) and (c) of Act No. 431/2002 Coll. on Accounting are retained for 10 years following the year to which they relate. After the expiry of the relevant statutory period, the personal data are erased or the relevant documents are securely destroyed, unless a special legal regulation or ongoing proceedings require their further retention.
- **Transfer of personal data to third countries or international organisations:** When accounting documents are sent or received electronically via the e-mail service provider Namecheap, Inc. (USA), personal data may be transferred to the United States of America. The relevant transfers are safeguarded by standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission pursuant to Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR, which form part of the provider's contractual terms.

### Exercise, demonstration and defence of legal claims

In connection with the operation of the Application and the provision of services, we may process personal data necessary to protect our rights and legitimate interests, in particular when resolving contractual disputes, asserting receivables, investigating breaches of contractual obligations or defending legal claims asserted against the Company.

For this purpose, we process personal data to the extent necessary to demonstrate the decisive facts, assess the legitimacy of a specific claim and ensure its out-of-court or judicial resolution.

- **Purpose of processing:** Exercise, demonstration and defence of the rights and legal claims of the Company, resolution of contractual disputes, recovery of receivables, investigation of breaches of contractual obligations, handling of legal submissions and ensuring the related out-of-court, judicial or administrative proceedings.
- **Legal basis:** Legitimate interest pursuant to Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The Company's main legitimate interest is the protection of its rights and property interests, ensuring the fulfilment of contractual obligations, the assertion of legitimate claims and effective defence against claims asserted against the Company.
- **Categories of data subjects:** Customers who are self-employed natural persons (sole traders), persons acting on behalf of Customers, Users of the Application, self-employed natural persons (sole traders) and other natural persons whose personal data are relevant to a specific legal claim or dispute.
- **Categories of personal data:** Identification and contact details, data on the contractual relationship and its performance, user account identifiers, data on the acceptance of contractual documents, relevant contractual and electronic communication, data on receivables, payables and payments, records of decisive actions, data on claims asserted, related legal submissions, decisions and measures taken. Depending on the nature of the specific claim, also other personal data necessary to prove, assert or defend it
- **Categories of recipients:** Designated persons of the Company, attorneys and other professional service providers involved in resolving legal claims, the competent courts, bailiffs and other authorised public authorities. When storing or making available relevant electronic records, also the technical service providers Supabase Pte. Ltd., Vercel Inc. and the e-mail provider Namecheap, Inc., to the extent of their actual involvement.
- **Erasure period:** Personal data necessary to protect legal claims arising from the contractual relationship are retained for a maximum of 5 years from its termination or from the final settlement of the related obligations, whichever occurs later. For other legal claims, personal data are retained for a maximum of 5 years from the occurrence of the decisive fact giving rise to the relevant claim. If a specific legal claim is asserted or relevant proceedings are initiated within that period, the personal data necessary for its resolution are retained until the final and binding conclusion of the proceedings and the full settlement of the related claims. Personal data whose further retention is not necessary for these purposes are erased or anonymised. This is without prejudice to special statutory retention periods.
- **Transfer of personal data to third countries or international organisations:** When storing or making available electronic records via the providers Supabase Pte. Ltd. (Singapore) and Vercel Inc. (USA), personal data may be transferred to Singapore and the United States of America, while the main database infrastructure and server functions of the Application are located in Germany. In the case of electronic communication via the provider Namecheap, Inc. (USA), personal data may also be processed in the USA. To safeguard the relevant transfers, the providers use standard contractual clauses pursuant to Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR.
